Before you buy

What to check on any property

Requirements vary by state and by type of property. These are the points we help our buyers look at.

  • Title and ownership. The seller's right to sell, supported by title documents.
  • Encumbrances. No loans, disputes or claims on the property.
  • Land use and approvals. The land is approved for the intended use, and layouts or buildings are approved by the local authority.
  • RERA registration. For eligible projects, check the registration number on your state's RERA website.
  • Revenue records. Mutation and land records are up to date and match the seller's name.
  • Registration costs. Stamp duty and registration charges differ by state. Ask for the exact figure up front.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For a major purchase, consider having your own lawyer review the documents.

Buyer FAQ

Good questions to ask

Is a plot or a built home better for me?

A plot gives you design freedom and often a lower entry price, but you will need to plan construction. A built home is ready sooner. We can talk through both based on your budget and timeline.

Can I get a home loan?

Most lenders finance approved property with clear documents. Eligibility, rates and terms are decided by the lender. We can guide you on the documents usually required.

Are there extra costs beyond the price?

Typically yes: stamp duty, registration fees and sometimes development or maintenance charges. These vary by state and property. Ask us for a full cost breakup before booking.

Can I visit before I decide?

Yes. Site visits are by appointment. Use the form above to request one.
